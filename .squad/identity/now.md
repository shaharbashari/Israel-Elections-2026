# What We're Focused On

The website is persisted in `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`, and `data.js`, with Hebrew usage and methodology documentation in `README.md`. Double-click `index.html`; no server, installation, account, remote assets, tracking, or automatic external requests are required.

The three-step questionnaire selects learning topics, evidence type, and reading depth. Results are universal and unranked, with fixed Hebrew alphabetical order, name-only search, public profiles, cited source details, and explicit comparison of up to four entities. No personal political views, values, demographics, voting intentions, alignment scores, or recommendations are collected or computed. Inputs stay in browser memory and reset on reload.

The research snapshot contains 15 organizations/formations, 11 public profiles, and 40 source URL records, of which 25 support nonmissing evidence. Its cutoff is 2026-10-07. This is curated, non-exhaustive coverage containing historical, undated, uncertain, and missing evidence. The claimed 2026-10-27 election date, approved lists, and current candidacies remain unverified. Source timestamps are recorded session reference times, not independently audited individual HTTP-request times.
